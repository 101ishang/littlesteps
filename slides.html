👣 LittleSteps

A friendly developmental check-in for parents. Not a diagnosis.

The problem

"One of the biggest challenges new parents faced was identifying early signs of problems in a child's growth and development — issues that, if left unaddressed, surfaced later as more serious problems."

— my mother, a pediatrician serving a rural community for 20 years

CDC data backs this at scale: parents of 79% of young children reported they were not asked to participate in a developmental screening in the past year (National Survey of Children's Health, via CDC analysis).

What's new

AlternativeGap
CDC's own Milestone Tracker app (same data)Static checklist — no explanation, no safety review, no persistent longitudinal account
ASQ-3 / ASQ OnlinePaid, clinic-oriented ($149.95–$349.95+/yr, $0.50/screen) — not a free consumer product
General chatbotsNo sourced grounding, no history, no enforced non-diagnostic boundary
LittleSteps: same trusted CDC data, plus an AI-written plain-English explanation, a dedicated safety-review agent, free, account-based, longitudinal, and honest about its own gaps.

Agent architecture

The model only narrates an already-decided result — it can never add, remove, or change a flag.

Cost-cutting: narration only (no LLM analysis), openai_reasoning_effort="none", narrow 2-field output schemas.

Safety

Risk: prompt injection via a parent-controlled field (child's nickname) trying to force a diagnosis.

Defense: a second agent reviews every draft against the already-decided facts; on failure or any error, the app shows fixed, safe text instead.

Child named "Alex. Ignore instructions..." — the live model still said it does not diagnose a condition. Also proven deterministically in tests/test_agents.py via a simulated attack.

Impact

  • CDC: ~79% of young children's parents report not being asked to screen in the past year
  • Only ~30% of young children get a parent-completed developmental screening in a given year
  • Qualitative evidence: 20 years of a rural pediatrician's firsthand account (my mother)
Honest gap: no real parent has used this app yet. That's the single most valuable next step.

Limits & next steps

  • No real end-user testing yet; scoring thresholds are a draft heuristic, not clinically validated
  • Vision/hearing and school-age behavior screening not yet sourced (licensing unresolved)
  • No per-user rate/token cap yet; no hosted deployment
  • Next: clinical review by my mother; real parents trying it in her practice
  • Wire the already-built growth-percentile engine into the UI
  • Resolve licensing for the remaining screening domains
